UK Devolution Dashboard

Back to portfolio

Median gross weekly pay for full-time employees living in each area, 2008 to 2025, for five local authorities and Greater London. Every chart uses the same vertical scale, so the lines can be compared across charts.

Devolved governments and city-region mayors have varying powers over areas such as skills, transport and economic development, so differences in pay show the starting points they are working from.

Source: Office for National Statistics, Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings (resident analysis), via the Nomis API. London is the Greater London region; the other five are local authority areas.

Notes: figures are in current pounds (not adjusted for inflation). Estimates from 2011 use a different occupation weighting, so compare them with earlier years with care.
