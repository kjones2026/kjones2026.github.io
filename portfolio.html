CC4. Replication. Re-create, then improve, someone else's chart

Original chart

Source: Uppsala Conflict Data Program (2026); geoBoundaries (2023), processed by Our World in Data. CC BY 4.0.

This is the chart I took from Our World in Data, showing deaths in armed conflicts by region from 1989 to 2026. The issues I recognise are:

  1. The 2026 bar covers only the first half of the year but looks like deaths have collapsed.
  2. Stacking means only the bottom region shares a baseline, so it is hard to compare Europe, the Middle East and Asia.
  3. The tan and salmon colours are close in lightness, which can be hard for colour-blind readers.
  4. The title names the topic without stating a finding.

Replicated chart

I replicated the chart by downloading the CSV from Our World in Data and saving it to my GitHub repository. The dates did not need amending, as the data only contains years rather than full dates. I renamed the deaths column to "deaths" for simplicity. I then wrote a Vega-Lite JSON file in VS Code (cc4_replica.json) that loads the CSV, filters it to four regions, and tidies the region names. It uses a stacked bar mark, with Americas at the bottom, and I styled it to match the original: white background, serif title, dashed grid lines, alternate-year labels and similar colours. I embedded it with vegaEmbed.

Improved chart

I copied the replica's JSON, changed the mark to line, filtered to 2000+, added layers for dashed 2026 and direct labels, and rewrote the title.
